2026 Golf Visa — Terms and Conditions

  • Each card includes four 2-for-1 green fees at each participating course. 2-for-1 offers also apply at participating indoor golf facilities — see the MYGV app for details.
  • After redeeming all four at a course, receive one free green fee (single player) with power cart rental at that course. At each Indoor Golf: after redeeming all of your 2-for-1 simulator plays, receive one free 1-hour simulator play. One reward per card, per course and indoor golf facility.
  • Valid until the end of each participating course’s 2026 season. End-of-season dates vary by course. For indoor simulators, valid until December 31, 2026.
  • Not valid for tournaments or organized events of any kind. Indoor golf: not valid for multi-bay bookings or large parties — refer to the MYGV app for further details.
  • May not apply to twilight rates at some courses; courses may base the 2-for-1 on rack rates. Visit course details in the MYGV app and click the green fee rates link.
  • GOLF VISA can’t be combined with other discount promotions. If you’re a member at one of these courses you cannot use your GVNS card as one of the 2 players in order to get a free for the second player in your twosome at your home course.
  • Please mention you are using your GOLF VISA when booking and present your card to the pro shop attendant at check in.
  • Maximum two 2-for-1 redemptions (four players) per card, per booking. If a course requires two cards per foursome; it will be mentioned on the course’s details page in the MYGV app or on the GV website.
  • Tee times subject to availability. The times from which you can book are listed on each course’s details page in the MYGV app or on the GV website.
  • You can share your GVNB card.
