About the Tournament

Join us for the inaugural GOLF VISA Charity Scramble — a day of golf, giving back, and great company. Proceeds from this event will support local youth development programs in the Caracas community.

Formatted as a 4-person scramble, this tournament welcomes golfers of all skill levels. Whether you're a seasoned pro or picking up a club for the first time, you'll enjoy a memorable day on one of Venezuela's premier courses.

Last year's event raised over $25,000 for charity. This year, we aim even higher.
