Accessibility

MYGV is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for all users.

Our Commitment

We strive to make our website accessible to everyone, regardless of ability or technology. We follow WCAG 2.1 Level AA guidelines to ensure a seamless experience.

Accessibility Features
  • Semantic HTML structure for screen reader compatibility
  • Keyboard-navigable menus and interactive elements
  • High contrast ratios for readability
  • Alt text on all images
  • Responsive design for various screen sizes
Feedback

If you encounter any accessibility barriers, please contact us. We aim to respond within 48 hours.
