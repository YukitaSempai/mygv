About

Mission Statement

To connect discerning golfers with Canada's most extraordinary courses through a seamless, premium membership experience.

MYGV was founded with a single purpose: to elevate the golf experience by removing barriers, curating excellence, and building a community of passionate players. We believe that every round should be memorable, every fairway should inspire, and every member should feel valued.

Our values: Integrity, Excellence, Community, and a deep respect for the game and the land it's played on.
