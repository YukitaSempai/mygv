Special Directions to Aroostook Valley Country Club

Aroostook Valley Country Club is located in Four Falls, New Brunswick. Please follow these special directions.

From Perth-Andover

  1. Head west towards Edmundston / Grand Falls
  2. Take Exit #107, then at the bottom of the ramp turn left
  3. You will go under two overpasses and immediately after going under the second overpass turn left again — the sign will say Four Falls Highway 130
  4. You will find yourself on a large bridge that crosses the Aroostook River; immediately at the end of the bridge turn left on Brown Road
  5. Continue on Brown Road straight west towards the Maine / NB border
  6. Brown Road will come to an end and turn into Russell Road Fort Fairfield; you will see a modular building which was a Canadian Customs
  7. 200 yards before that building turn left on the field road — it is clearly marked
  8. Continue to our parking area where you will see carts parked outside of a large dome building
  9. Hop on a cart and follow signs to the club house

From Grand Falls

  1. Head east towards Perth-Andover
  2. Take Exit #99, then turn right onto Highway 130 towards Four Falls
  3. Before the large bridge that crosses the Aroostook River, immediately turn right on Brown Road
  4. Continue on Brown Road straight west towards the Maine / NB border
  5. Brown Road will come to an end and turn into Russell Road Fort Fairfield; you will see a modular building which was a Canadian Customs
  6. 200 yards before that building turn left on the field road — it is clearly marked
  7. Continue to our parking area where you will see carts parked outside of a large dome building
  8. Hop on a cart and follow signs to the club house
