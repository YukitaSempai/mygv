Our Mission at MYGV

Our mission is to connect golfers and courses through a platform that delivers lasting value, meaningful experiences, and measurable results. Through innovation and trusted partnerships, we enhance accessibility, deepen engagement, and help the game of golf continue to grow\u2014both on and off the course.

Trusted Partnerships

Building strong relationships with courses and golfers across the Maritimes.

Innovation

Continuously improving our platform to deliver measurable value for everyone.

Growing the Game

Making golf more accessible and enjoyable for players of all levels.
